04 / SPEND · CURRENT FOCUS

Which observation is worth paying for?

In a clinical study, an outcome may not have happened when follow-up ends. We know something, but not everything. I study how to spend a limited budget on the observations that matter most.

Explore this work ↓
The idea, more precisely +

Choose follow-up decisions S to maximize their information value, subject to a budget B.

maximize I(S)   subject to   Σ cost(i) ≤ B

In my master’s thesis, a maximum-coverage formulation gives a (1 − 1/e) approximation guarantee for the corresponding batch selection problem.
