Spend Compute Where Errors Propagate

Where should another unit of computation go?

Spend Compute Where Errors Propagate

Ali Parsaee, Yashar Talebirad
First author

When a hard problem is split into dependent sub-problems, a mistake high in the structure corrupts everything built on it. Extra computation should go where errors propagate.

Simulation of the idea on small dependency trees. Sub-problem error falls with more samples; a wrong answer corrupts everything below it.

This manuscript is under review. This page explains the question and the idea; results will be added once the paper is public.

The problem

AI systems often break a problem into a plan, sub-plans and steps, sample several answers at each step, and aggregate them by voting or with a verifier. In common practice every step gets the same number of samples, whatever depends on it.

What we did

The project formalizes the expected amount of corrupted work and derives how a fixed sampling budget should be divided across the dependency structure.

What we found

  • The results are under review and will be posted when the paper is public.
Go deeper

Whether structure-aware allocation helps depends on how errors are scored. If any single mistake ruins the whole answer, the structure stops mattering; if partial work keeps its value, it matters a great deal.
